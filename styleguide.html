Motion

Add data-reveal to fade an element up as it scrolls into view (="left", ="right" or ="zoom" for other directions), or data-reveal-stagger to a list so its items follow one after another. main.js runs it. Nothing is hidden without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion see everything still. Hover effects (button lift, arrow nudge, card lift, photo zoom) are plain CSS.
