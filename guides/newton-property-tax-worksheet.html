Newton Property Tax Worksheet

Newton's tax rate is easy to find. What it means for a specific home, and when the bills actually arrive, takes a little more work. This worksheet walks through the formula, the value to use, Newton's quarterly bill calendar, and what to check before and after you buy. It ends with two pages to fill in for each home you're considering.

What's inside

  • The formula for Newton's yearly property tax, with the FY2026 and FY2025 residential rates and a worked example
  • Assessed value versus purchase price, and how to look up a home's assessed value
  • Newton's quarterly tax bill calendar, the abatement window, and why to keep paying while you apply
  • What to check if you just bought a home, or are about to
  • Newton in context: average single-family values and tax bills from the Massachusetts Division of Local Services
  • A Tax Math Worksheet and a First-Year Budget, one copy per home
  • Questions to ask Newton Assessing, your lender, your attorney, and the listing agent
