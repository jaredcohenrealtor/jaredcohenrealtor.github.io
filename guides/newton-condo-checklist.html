Condo or Single-Family? The Newton First-Time Buyer's Condo Checklist

Documents to request, questions to ask, and a side-by-side cost worksheet.

Choosing between a condo and a single-family home in Newton isn't just about price. With a condo, you're also buying into a building's finances, rules, and decisions. With a single-family home, every repair is yours alone. This checklist helps you compare the two once every cost is on the table.

  • Planning for repairs if you choose a single-family home
