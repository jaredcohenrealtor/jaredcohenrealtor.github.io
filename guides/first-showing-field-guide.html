First Showing Field Guide

A showing goes by fast, and it's easy to leave without ever looking at the basement. This field guide gives you a route through the house, what to look at in each room, and the questions to ask before you leave. Bring it to any showing or open house. It isn't a substitute for a professional home inspection.

What's inside

  • What to bring to a showing
  • A room-by-room route through the house that takes about 20 minutes
  • What's usually cosmetic, and what's worth asking your home inspector about
  • What to notice in older Newton homes, and the Massachusetts lead paint notice for homes built before 1978
  • A Room-by-Room Checklist and a Car Debrief, one copy per home
  • Questions to ask before you leave: about the home, the sale, and condos
