Newton Transportation Guide

Thinking about a move to Newton, or already here and want to rely less on the car? This guide pulls together the public transit and bike options that serve Newton's villages, so you can see at a glance how you'd get to work, school, or into Boston from a given neighborhood.

What's inside

  • The Green Line D (Riverside) branch and its Newton stops, from Riverside to Chestnut Hill
  • Commuter rail on the Framingham/Worcester Line at Auburndale, West Newton, and Newtonville
  • MBTA bus routes serving Newton: express to Back Bay and Downtown, connections to Kenmore, Newton Corner routes, and cross-suburban routes
  • The RIDE, the MBTA's door-to-door paratransit service
  • MBTA reduced-fare programs, and who rides free
  • MetroWest Regional Transit Authority service west of Newton, including Route 1 from Woodland Station
  • Bluebikes bikeshare stations in Newton
